# Cider Beta Social Community Design

## Goal

Create a small pre-beta social presence for Cider that gives early users a clear place to discuss the app, report bugs, suggest ideas, and follow beta progress.

The tone should match the existing Cider brand: native, clean, focused, premium, and calm. The community should feel useful and approachable, not oversized before launch.

## Scope

This design covers:

- Discord server structure and launch copy
- Channel descriptions and pinned starter posts
- Bug and idea templates
- Pre-beta X posts
- Reddit post drafts

It does not cover automated Discord bots, paid social campaigns, analytics dashboards, or a large moderation system.

## Discord Approach

Use a restrained beta hub with only the channels needed for early discussion.

### Start Here

- `#welcome`: what Cider is, current beta status, useful links
- `#announcements`: beta builds, release notes, important updates
- `#rules`: short community guidelines

### Community

- `#general`: everyday discussion about using Cider
- `#questions`: help and usage questions
- `#show-your-setup`: optional workflow screenshots and organization ideas

### Feedback

- `#ideas`: feature ideas and workflow suggestions
- `#bugs`: bug reports using a simple template
- `#known-issues`: current known issues and workarounds
- `#beta-feedback`: broader impressions, rough edges, and beta discussion

This structure can shrink if needed by merging `#questions` into `#general` and `#beta-feedback` into `#ideas`.

## Social Approach

X should use concise maker-style posts that explain what Cider is, why it exists, and what beta testers can help shape. Reddit should use transparent, community-specific posts that avoid launch hype and invite practical feedback.

## Success Criteria

- A new visitor immediately understands where to ask questions, report bugs, and suggest ideas.
- The Discord does not feel empty or overbuilt before beta.
- X posts clearly describe Cider without sounding like generic productivity marketing.
- Reddit posts are transparent about pre-beta status and ask for specific feedback.
